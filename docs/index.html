How it works

The whole thing in one sentence: a ballasted hinge, a linear actuator, an ESP32-S3, and a willingness to look at the weather.

Follows the sun

PI control loop on motor current. Tilts to commanded angle, stops on endstop spike.

Folds for wind

NWS forecast poll + on-board wind speed. Folds flat at 50% of safe motor current.

Auto-waters

Capacitive soil sensor + DS18B20. Waters when soil < 35% and battery > 11.5 V.
