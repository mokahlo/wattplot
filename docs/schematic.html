The 14 sections

Read left-to-right, top-to-bottom. Power tree at the top, ESP32-S3 in the middle, drivers and sensors at the bottom.

1.Power input (12 V)

XT60, 10 A ATO fuse, SS34 reverse-polarity, SMBJ16A TVS, bulk cap.

2.5 V buck (MP1584)

4.5–28 V → 5 V, 3 A peak. Feedback divider, catch diode, PWR LED.

3.3.3 V LDO (AMS1117)

1 A. Caps at ~1 A total — don't add a second relay without re-budgeting.

4.ESP32-S3

DevKitC-1-N16R8. USB-C, RST, BOOT, antenna keep-out, all GPIO.

5.USB-C + ESD

5.1 kΩ CC pull-downs, USBLC6-2SC6 ESD protection.

6.H-bridge #1 (DRV8871)

Actuator motor driver. 3.6 A peak, current sense, fault output.

7.H-bridge #2 (DRV8871)

Second DRV8871 in single-direction mode for the irrigation solenoid. nSLEEP tied high.

8.DS18B20 (1-Wire)

4.7 kΩ pull-up, ESD TVS, JST connector to probe. Three sensors in parallel.

9.Soil moisture

Capacitive sensor, low-pass filter, ESD TVS, 3-pin JST.

10.Battery V sense

100k / 10k divider (×11.0), 3.3 V clamp TVS, series resistor.

11.Current-based homing

v3.1 design: motor current spike (via IPROPI) detects endstop. Limit switches removed.

12.Status LED (PWM)

Monochromatic LED via LEDC PWM. Replaced RGB NeoPixel in v3.

13.I²C expansion

4-pin header, 4.7 kΩ pull-ups. INA219 (motor + panel), future IMU.

14.Title block

Project info, design summary, design rules, dependencies.
