The 14 sections

Read left-to-right, top-to-bottom. Power tree at the top, ESP32-S3 in the middle, drivers and sensors at the bottom.

01Power input (12 V)

XT60, 10 A ATO fuse, SS34 reverse-polarity, SMBJ16A TVS, bulk cap.

025 V buck (MP1584)

4.5–28 V → 5 V, 3 A peak. Feedback divider, catch diode, PWR LED.

033.3 V LDO (AMS1117)

1 A. Don't add a second relay without re-budgeting.

04ESP32-S3

DevKitC-1-N16R8. USB-C, RST, BOOT, antenna keep-out.

05USB-C + ESD

5.1 kΩ CC pull-downs, USBLC6-2SC6 ESD protection.

06H-bridge #1 (DRV8871)

Actuator motor. 3.6 A peak, current sense, fault output.

07H-bridge #2 (DRV8871)

Solenoid. Single-direction mode. nSLEEP tied high.

08DS18B20 (1-Wire)

4.7 kΩ pull-up, ESD TVS, JST. 3 sensors in parallel.

09Soil moisture

Capacitive, low-pass filter, ESD TVS, 3-pin JST.

10Battery V sense

100k / 10k divider (×11.0), 3.3 V clamp TVS.

11Current-based homing

v3.1: motor current spike (via IPROPI) detects endstop. Limit switches removed.

12Status LED (PWM)

Monochromatic LEDC PWM. Replaced RGB NeoPixel in v3.

13I²C expansion

4-pin header, 4.7 kΩ pull-ups. INA219 × 2, future IMU.

14Title block

Project info, design summary, design rules.
