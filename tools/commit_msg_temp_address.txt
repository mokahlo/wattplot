firmware: pin DS18B20 sensors by 64-bit ROM address

v3.1 left the dallas_temp components with no `address:` field, which
caused all three sensors to default to "the first sensor on the bus"
and report "Unable to select an address" at boot. The bus scan
worked (gpio.one_wire component listed both addresses correctly) but
the dallas_temp components couldn't disambiguate them.

Fix: pin each sensor by its unique 64-bit address, captured from the
bus scan log:

  - Panel Temperature       -> 0xb500000023e51028 (index 0 on bus)
  - Soil Temperature        -> 0x5a00000023417028 (index 1 on bus)
  - Canopy Air Temperature  -> 0x0000000000000000 (placeholder, NaN
                                                  until a third
                                                  sensor is wired)

Result: Panel reads 21.4 C, Soil reads 24.2 C, Canopy is NaN. The
two real sensors are independent (different temperatures) and the
firmware now knows which is which.
